How to read the results: win % is the model's chance for each bet, and fair odds are what that chance is worth with no bookmaker cut. Edge compares the model with the sportsbook's price. In testing, sportsbooks were more accurate than this model, so a very big edge usually means the model is missing news like an injury.

Winning margin

Team ratings give each team an offense and a defense score, in points above or below an average team, fitted to every score this season plus last season's scores at 20% weight. Home field is worth 1.5 points. The final margin and total are then treated as bell curves around the projection (13 points either side), each team's points likewise (9 points either side), and games tied after regulation are settled by an overtime field goal.

In a backtest over the 2024 and 2025 seasons these ratings were less accurate than bookmaker lines, so an "edge" here usually means the model is missing something, like injuries or a quarterback change. The model also ignores key numbers like 3 and 7, which matters most for alternate spreads.
